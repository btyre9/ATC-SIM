How to control an aircraft

First click an aircraft strip on the right. Then click MIC and say just one short instruction. You do not need to say the callsign again. If Chrome hears a word incorrectly, edit the text in the command bar and press SEND. You can still say a callsign and instruction together if you prefer.

Departures (blue strips) wait short of the runway until you clear them for takeoff. They climb to 5,000 ft on runway heading. Climb them to 10,000 ft or higher and turn them toward the exit gate on their strip; they hand off automatically when they cross the dashed 40 NM boundary. Arrivals that leave the boundary are lost.

In this simulator, “flight level three thousand” means 3,000 feet and “flight level twelve thousand” means 12,000 feet. “Flight level three zero zero” still means 30,000 feet. Say heading digits separately: “two seven zero” for 270°. ILS clearance requires an aircraft within 30 nautical miles of the runway.

Pilot voice

Each aircraft keeps its assigned voice throughout the session.

First use downloads about 100 MB. Chrome keeps the model and voices in its site cache; later visits load them into memory from there. If you clear site data or use another browser address, they may download again.

Press START KOKORO, or turn PILOTS on.

Pilot audio must be on to hear transmissions. Press TEST VOICE to hear your selection.
